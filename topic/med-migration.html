About this tracker

This tracker follows migration across the Mediterranean — the Aegean and the Greek islands, the central route to Italy and Malta, Libya and Tunisia — and the asylum and border policies of the EU, as the world's press reports them.

How ATHENA measures it

An event belongs to this tracker when its title names the topic, or when at least two of its reports — and at least a third of them — do. Risk is ATHENA's analytical indicator of the coverage (0–10), not a forecast.
