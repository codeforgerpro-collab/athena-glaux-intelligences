Explained

About this tracker

The shadow fleet is a few hundred older tankers with opaque owners and insurance that move sanctioned Russian, Iranian and Venezuelan oil; their condition and transfers at sea are a growing safety and sanctions risk.

How ATHENA measures it

An event belongs to this tracker when its title names the topic, or when at least two of its reports — and at least a third of them — do. Risk is ATHENA's analytical indicator of the coverage (0–10), not a forecast.
