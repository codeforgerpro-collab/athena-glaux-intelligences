About this tracker

The Western Balkans sit between the EU and the growing influence of Russia and China. This tracker follows Serbia–Kosovo relations, Bosnia and Herzegovina, EU enlargement, energy routes and regional trade as the world's press reports them.

How ATHENA measures it

An event belongs to this tracker when its title names the topic, or when at least two of its reports — and at least a third of them — do. Risk is ATHENA's analytical indicator of the coverage (0–10), not a forecast.
