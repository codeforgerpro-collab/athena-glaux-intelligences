Press release · For immediate release · 15 November 2026

The Strait of Hormuz carries the highest shipping risk in the first half of November at 6.8/10, ATHENA data show

ATHENA Maritime & Geopolitical Risk Report · 1–14 November 2026 · 17,500 events in 131 countries

ATHENA, Athens, 15 November 2026. ATHENA Glaux Intelligence, which reads more than 600 news outlets around the clock, recorded 17,500 events confirmed by at least two independent outlets in 131 countries between 1 and 14 November 2026. 223 of them were rated high-risk (6/10 or above), up 49% from 150 in the previous fortnight.

Maritime and shipping

Across the shipping chokepoints and routes ATHENA tracks, 143 events were reported, down 6% from 152 in the previous fortnight. The Strait of Hormuz carried the highest single risk score of the period (6.8/10), followed by Black Sea shipping (6.7) and Red Sea shipping (6.5). The highest-risk shipping event was: “Tankers hit in Hormuz as Iran keeps up pressure after US vote”, reported by 7 outlets.

At the time of release, ATHENA rated three chokepoints Disrupted (Bab el-Mandeb / Red Sea, Kerch Strait / Black Sea, Strait of Hormuz) and one chokepoint on Watch (Danish Straits / Baltic). Across all the waters it watches, 142 maritime incidents were reported in the period.

Geopolitics

The highest-risk geopolitical events of the period were:

  1. House Republicans Shoot Down Hegseth Impeachment Vote · risk 7.1 · 107 outlets · 41 countries
  2. Poland calls Krakow shooting rampage 'terrorist' attack · risk 7.5 · 56 outlets · 35 countries
  3. After JD Vance, now Trump Jr blames Iran war for US midterms defeat · risk 7.1 · 83 outlets · 39 countries
  4. Trump says Gulf allies urged restraint on Iran, admits war hurt Republicans in midterms · risk 6.8 · 106 outlets · 43 countries
  5. Democrats vow to rein in Trump after midterm victory · risk 7.1 · 57 outlets · 29 countries

Among the conflict and regional trackers: Russia–Ukraine war, 434 events (peak risk 7.2, up 19% from 365 in the previous fortnight); Israel, Gaza & the West Bank, 132 events (peak risk 6.9, up 63% from 81 in the previous fortnight); Eastern Mediterranean energy, 10 events (peak risk 6.0, up 100% from 5 in the previous fortnight).

“Over the past fifteen days the Strait of Hormuz carried the highest shipping risk we recorded, at 6.8 out of 10,” said Antonios Vlachos, founder of ATHENA Glaux Intelligence. “Every figure in this report comes from events that at least two independent outlets reported, and each one links to its sources on our site.”

About the data: counts cover the events active between 1 and 14 November 2026, each confirmed by at least two independent outlets. The risk score is ATHENA's 0-10 indicator of how serious the reported developments are, based on what the outlets report; it is not a forecast. Comparisons are with the fortnight before.

About ATHENA Glaux Intelligence: ATHENA (athenaglauxintelligence.news) is an independent news-intelligence service based in Athens and published by Tekmerion. It reads more than 600 news outlets around the clock, groups their reports into events, and publishes an event only when at least two separate outlets report it. Every event carries a risk, importance and confidence score, and every figure links to its sources.

ATHENA Glaux Intelligence est un service de veille et d'analyse de l'actualité (news intelligence), pas un journal : il rassemble les reportages d'autres rédactions, les regroupe en événements et les explique par des chiffres. Ses textes courts sont rédigés automatiquement à partir des articles liés, et chaque événement mène à ses sources d'origine. Ce que vous voyez

  • Les événements vérifiés les plus importants du moment, avec leur risque (0–10) et le nombre de médias qui les rapportent.

Comment l'utiliser

  • Cliquez un titre pour l'événement complet : sources, pays, historique du risque.
  • Le menu mène aux rubriques, à la Carte mentale, au Local et au Maritime.

Couleurs du risque : or sous 4 · ambre 4–6 · orange 6–8 · rouge 8 et plus (échelle 0–10).

ATHENA ne publie un événement que lorsqu'au moins deux médias indépendants le rapportent.
