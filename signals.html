How to read a signal

Sudden surge
Far more reports than usual about a name, a country or a topic.
New name in the news
A name that was almost absent from the news is suddenly widely reported.
Rising risk
The risk score of one event climbed quickly.
Contradictory reporting
Outlets report claims about the same event that dispute each other.

Signals count English-language reports from the outlets ATHENA follows. A rise in coverage is not proof of a rise in what happens on the ground.
