How the Analysis Desk works

What the desk covers

Security and conflict stories reported in the last 7 days by at least 3 independent outlets. Anything else is left to the other sections of the site.

From headline to score

  1. Collect: reports from monitored outlets are grouped into one story when they describe the same event.
  2. Classify: each story is placed in a section and checked for the words and actors that signal severity.
  3. Score: risk, confidence and source reliability are computed by rule, as below.
  4. Publish: the score, the evidence and the limits go on the page; each change is logged with its date.

What is read

The factors below are read for every story, listed in no particular order. How they are weighed and combined is not published here. It is provided to anyone who asks: write to info@athenaglauxintelligence.news.

Risk (0 to 10)

Built from six factors and scaled to 10.

  • Cascade (spill-over into other areas)
  • Evidence quality
  • Reach (countries and outlets)
  • Reader exposure
  • Severity of reported events
  • Urgency (how recent, how fast)

Confidence

How far the evidence supports the score. Several independent outlets in several countries raise it.

  • Clarity of classification
  • Countries reporting
  • Independent outlets
  • Reliability of the outlets
  • Stability over time

Source reliability

How much weight one outlet's report carries.

  • Corroboration by others
  • Independence from each other
  • Original reporting
  • Provenance
  • Reported first
  • Stability over time
  • Technical soundness

White paper

Provided by ATHENA Glaux Intelligence: the scope, principles, governance and checking of the Desk, in a form that can be cited. The full method is available on request. English, PDF.

The instruments

Each story card carries these charts. The track record page adds four more.

  • Nemesis line: What would prove the reading wrong
  • Argus watch: How the coverage built up
  • Scales of Themis: What weighs on the risk score
  • Pythia's ladder: The four paths, from the most to the least reported
  • Hermes' routes: Where in the world it is reported
  • Aletheia's balance: Whether the reporting is one-sided

Risk bands

Lowbelow 4
Moderate4 to under 6
Elevated6 to under 8
Critical8 and above

A score is a position on this scale, not a probability.

Confidence on the cards

A card shows higher confidence only when at least 15 outlets in at least 3 countries report the story. Otherwise it shows low confidence, even if the risk is high.

What would change the score

Each card lists a trigger: the reading is judged to have moved when the risk score changes by 2.0 points or more, up or down, inside 14 days.

What the desk cannot do

  • It reads reports, not events on the ground. If the reports are wrong or one-sided, the score can be too.
  • It does not read intentions and does not predict single decisions by named people.
  • It gives no percentages, because there is not yet a track record long enough to calibrate them. The record page shows how that record is building.
  • It is not advice for travel, trade or investment.

Review and credit

The framework was designed with the academic guidance of Professor Dimitrios Thomakos. The scores themselves are produced by ATHENA's rules and are not reviewed by him or by any human analyst before publication.
