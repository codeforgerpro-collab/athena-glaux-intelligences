  • The rolling bar under the menu carries breaking events; the boxes further down show today's briefing, risk trackers and warnings.

How to use it

  • Click a headline for the full event: sources, countries, risk history.
  • Use the menu for sections, the Mind Graph, Local news and Maritime.

Risk colours: gold under 4 · amber 4–6 · orange 6–8 · red 8 and above (scale 0–10).

ATHENA publishes an event only when at least two independent outlets report it.
